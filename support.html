Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my Strava account?

When you first open BikeCheck, tap "Sign in with Strava" and authorize the app to access your profile and activities. BikeCheck only requests the minimum permissions needed for bike maintenance tracking.

Why aren't all my bikes showing up?

BikeCheck pulls your gear list directly from your Strava profile, so any bike added to Strava should appear. If a bike is missing, make sure it's added to your Strava gear and try signing out and back in to refresh your data.

How are service intervals calculated?

Service intervals are based on actual ride time (moving time) from your Strava activities. When you create a service interval, BikeCheck tracks the cumulative ride time on that bike and notifies you when maintenance is due.

Can I use BikeCheck without Strava?

Currently, BikeCheck requires Strava integration to track your bikes and activities. This ensures accurate maintenance scheduling based on your actual riding patterns.

How do I reset a service interval?

Open the service interval details and tap "Reset Interval". This resets the timer based on your current total ride time, indicating you've performed the maintenance.

Why am I not receiving notifications?

Make sure you've enabled notifications in your device settings for BikeCheck, and check that the "Notify" toggle is on for the service interval in question. Also note that BikeCheck intentionally limits reminders to once per component per week — if you received one recently, the next one will come after 7 days.

What is "Mark as Needs Service"?

This lets you immediately flag any component as overdue — regardless of where it is in its normal service cycle. It's useful when you notice something needs attention before the timer runs out (a worn chain, a spongy brake, a creaking bottom bracket). The flag is cleared when you reset the interval after servicing.

How does Smart Bike Detection work?

When you add a bike, BikeCheck checks it against a database of 220+ models to identify your bike type — road, gravel, hardtail, full-suspension, etc. It then suggests the most relevant service intervals for that type, so you don't have to start from scratch. You can always adjust or add more intervals manually.

What is the Shop Assistant?

The Shop Assistant is an AI-powered bike mechanic built into BikeCheck. You can ask it questions about servicing specific components — torque specs, bleed procedures, how often to lube your chain, when to replace brake pads, and more. It runs entirely on-device using Apple Intelligence, so nothing you type is sent to a server. Available on iOS 26 and later.

How do I share a bike maintenance report?

Open the bike detail view, tap the overflow menu (···), and choose "Share Bike Report." BikeCheck generates a formatted summary of all service intervals, their current status, and reset history that you can share via Messages, Mail, AirDrop, or any other app.

Is my data secure?

Your service intervals, maintenance history, and bike data are stored locally on your device. BikeCheck uses anonymous, privacy-preserving analytics (via TelemetryDeck) to understand how the app is used — no personally identifiable information is included. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Getting Started

  1. Connect Strava: Sign in with your Strava account
  2. Sync Data: BikeCheck will import your bikes and recent activities
  3. Create Service Intervals: Set up maintenance schedules for bike components
  4. Get Notified: Receive reminders when maintenance is due
  5. Reset When Done: Mark maintenance as complete to reset the timer

Troubleshooting

App is not syncing with Strava

  • Check your internet connection
  • Try logging out and back in to Strava
  • Make sure BikeCheck has permission to access your Strava data

Service intervals seem incorrect

  • Verify your activities are properly categorized as "Ride" in Strava
  • Check that the correct bike was selected for each activity
  • Manual activities entered in Strava may not have accurate moving time
