BikeCheck

The smartest way to keep your bike running at its best

🚴‍♂️ Strava Integration

Connect your Strava account and BikeCheck automatically imports your bikes and syncs your recent rides. Your actual moving time drives every maintenance calculation — no guesswork.

⏰ Service Intervals

Set maintenance schedules for any component — chain, fork, brake pads, rear shock, and more. Choose from 17 pre-built part templates with sensible defaults, or dial in your own intervals.

🔍 Smart Bike Detection

BikeCheck identifies your bike type from a database of 220+ models and automatically suggests the right service intervals for how you ride — whether it's a cross-country hardtail or a full-suspension enduro rig.

🚦 Bike Health at a Glance

Every bike shows a live status — Good, Needs Service, or Overdue — based on the health of its components. You can also manually flag any part as needing service right now, bypassing the timer entirely.

🔔 Smart Notifications

Get reminded when maintenance is due without being spammed. BikeCheck sends at most one notification per component per week, so you only hear from it when it matters.

📊 Activity Tracking

See your recent rides, per-bike mileage, total hours in the saddle, and how that usage stacks up against each component's service schedule. Your riding stats live on your profile.

🛠️ Maintenance History

Every reset logs a service record with the date and an optional note. Share a full maintenance report for any bike — handy when selling, getting a tune-up, or just keeping records.

🤖 Shop Assistant

Not sure when to service your fork or how to bleed your brakes? Ask the built-in AI bike mechanic. Powered entirely on-device by Apple Intelligence — your conversations never leave your phone. (iOS 26+)

📱 Cross-Platform

Native apps for both iOS and Android, each built for its platform. Your bikes, your service history, your maintenance schedule — wherever you ride.
