Installing
----------
This is a sideload build, not a Play Store release. To install:

1. Download BikeCheck-1.0-beta.apk to your phone.
2. Open it. Android will ask permission to install apps from this
   source — allow it for your browser or file manager.
3. Tap Install.

Because the app is not distributed through the Play Store, Android may
show a Play Protect warning. Choosing "Install anyway" is expected for
sideloaded apps.


What's included
---------------
- Strava sign-in, with automatic sync of your bikes and recent activities
- Service interval tracking per bike, with hours-based wear calculation
- Smart bike detection and part presets — pick your bike, get sensible
  starting intervals
- 17 part templates across drivetrain, suspension, wheels, brakes and
  cockpit
- Mark as Needs Service, to flag a part as due regardless of ride hours
- Service history with reset records and notes
- Activity detail with route map and segment efforts (KOM/PR badges)
- Nearby bike shops, with hand-off to your maps app for directions
- Share a formatted service report for any bike
- Local notifications for overdue service, throttled to once a week
- Background sync of new Strava rides
- Profile stats: bikes, miles, hours, activities, parts tracked,
  services logged, overdue count


Not on Android
--------------
Shop Assistant, the AI mechanic chat, is iOS-only. It runs on Apple's
on-device Foundation Models and has no Android equivalent in this
release.

Cloud sync is likewise iOS-only in v1.


Known limitations
-----------------
- Beta software. Please back up nothing you can't recreate — there is
  no data export or restore yet.
- Bike type detection matches on bike name only. If your Strava gear
  has an unusual name, pick the parts manually.
- Route maps use OpenStreetMap tiles and need a network connection.
